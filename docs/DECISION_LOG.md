# Decision log and open questions

## Status vocabulary

- **Confirmed requirement:** specified by the project owner; not an implementation claim.
- **Proposed:** recommended for owner and pilot review; can change after evidence.
- **Open:** decision requires owner/participant input or pilot validation.

Proposing a technical structure does not resolve a participant-dependent business rule. D-09, D-16 through D-25 remain proposals until owner/pilot review and their follow-up questions are answered. A database mechanism can be specified without deciding who may exercise it or whether the represented practice is acceptable locally.

## Unresolved business questions

### Pilot definition and participation

1. Which location, site(s), holes, Doras, merchants, carriers and recovery facility are in the first pilot? Who owns product decisions and day-to-day support?
2. Which participants consent to record data and share it with counterparties? What languages, devices, literacy/accessibility needs and network conditions should be supported?
3. Is a cooperative, association or other organization present? Participation must remain optional unless the pilot confirms otherwise.

### Production and material handling

4. What does a production event represent locally: a work period, batch, worker allocation, or another unit? Who records and acknowledges it?
5. How are beneficiaries and allocations determined and updated? Which cases are disputed or provisional?
6. What are the practical batch boundaries for soil/feed, gold, residue and stockpiles? How are lots labeled and physically segregated or mixed?
7. When and how does a Dora's retained residue become service consideration? Can terms differ by participant or event? How is that recorded without implying a universal rule?
8. What inputs/outputs and measurement variance does a pilot need at a processing and recovery event? What is intentionally not measured?
9. Is a defensible allocation method needed after residue pooling? If so, who selects and validates it, and what evidence supports it?

### Measurement, trade and custody

10. Which weight units, decimal precision, gross/net/tare conventions, moisture handling and weighing instruments are used at each transfer?
11. Who may create a measured or “verified” record? What verification evidence is sufficient for this product's purpose?
12. Which assay methods/results are used, who performs them, what does the counterparty accept, and how should re-assays/disputes be recorded?
13. What currency, price basis and payment states/methods should the pilot represent? Are partial payments/common adjustments in scope?
14. What constitutes an offered, accepted or disputed custody transfer? Which party's signature is required at production, Dora delivery, consignment dispatch/receipt and merchant sale?
15. How much of source identity and commercial terms should a buyer see for a pooled or merchant-aggregated batch?

### Governance, privacy and operations

16. What evidence may be collected for landowner/permit-holder relationships, and who is allowed to see it? No permit or title verification is assumed.
17. Which fields are personal, commercially sensitive or confidential to a participant? What are access, retention, export, correction, deletion and backup rules?
18. Who approves/revokes user roles and investigates a disputed record? What is the escalation path if a party cannot access a device or connectivity?
19. Is independent verification genuinely required for the pilot? Which organizations would run network nodes, hold keys and approve membership/policy/contract upgrades? How are costs and outages shared?
20. What hosting region, support hours, recovery target, data export and exit process should the pilot owner require?
21. Who may propose, dispute, approve or correct production allocations? Is an allocation quantity-based or share-based for a given output? Does review apply to a complete set or may one beneficiary row be disputed independently? Which observation kinds (`DECLARED`, `MEASURED`, `VERIFIED`) are eligible, who is authorized to select the exact basis observation, what evidence/reason is required for a conflict, and how are rounding and intentionally unallocated quantities handled? The database has no latest-wins rule; this does not select the local policy.
22. Can each relevant physical stockpile, consignment and facility/custodian inventory account be represented as a location? Who records dispatch, receipt and counts? What should happen when measured receipt differs from dispatch: leave quantity in transit, approve a variance, or another process? Who authorizes opening balances, adjustments and reversals, and what evidence is required? Who may approve a reversal that reduces net commercial OUT after a dispute or quantity correction?
23. What exactly does the initial transaction quantity represent in each deal? Who accepts the final trade quantity, can a sale be partially delivered, and what does the pilot use for canonical mass units, source-unit conversions, rounding and instrument precision? If an accepted quantity is corrected below already moved material, who approves the needed compensating reversal and how is that commercial correction resolved?
24. How can a participant with no smartphone review/acknowledge a record? Which authorized users may assist or transcribe paper forms, what evidence is acceptable, how are refusals/disputes handled, and what is recorded if acknowledgement is unavailable?
25. What evidence confirms a payment? Who can confirm/reverse it? How are fees, partial reversals, overpayment, cash claims and reconciliation disputes treated? Which currencies and currency minor-unit/rounding rules are supported? When a wrong-currency parent is superseded, how are existing payment claims, confirmations and any settlement against the predecessor reconciled? Currency conversion is excluded from the proposed MVP; is it needed later, and who authorizes its rate/evidence?
26. Which records define an audit aggregate stream when one command affects a batch, trade, inventory location and consignment? What checkpoint frequency and independent holder are practical, and who owns/checks signing keys?
27. For inventory held at one physical place by a new custodian, is a distinct custodian inventory account plus balanced transfer appropriate? Who accepts custody and records that transfer? This remains separate from ownership and entitlement.

## Assumptions requiring pilot validation

- Participants can consistently identify a batch and relate it to a site/hole or processing/recovery activity.
- At least some counterparties are willing to enter or acknowledge shared transaction records.
- Offline-first command capture is more useful than paper-first capture followed by later entry; this should be tested directly.
- Participants without personal smartphones can be represented through an authorized recorder or approved paper transcription without treating recorder entry as participant acknowledgement.
- Physical dispatch and receipt can be mapped to source/destination inventory locations and paired journal legs; count adjustments can be separately evidenced.
- All application writers will use the same PostgreSQL transaction and `inventory_balance_guard` locking protocol. A bypassing writer or direct database edit would invalidate the nonnegative-balance guarantee.
- Ordinary application roles cannot change parent transaction currency; a sufficiently privileged PostgreSQL owner/superuser can alter schema or disable triggers, so privileged access must be restricted and audited.
- Commercial OUT caps serialize on the parent/line chain before inventory guard locks and audit-stream locks. Accepted line corrections, reversal authority and the participant process remain to be approved; this design is not evidence that the process has been agreed.
- A same actor-scoped idempotency key makes an offline retry idempotent. The system cannot infer that two separately keyed submissions describe the same physical transfer without a shared operation reference or human reconciliation; the pilot must decide how to supply or review that identity.
- Allocation basis authorization is proposed to require an approval event from a user holding a pilot-authorized reviewer role; the proposal alone is not approval. Whether one person may hold both proposer and reviewer roles, and which measurement kinds qualify, remain unresolved.
- A wrong-currency successor leaves the predecessor's payment claims/events attached to the predecessor; the pilot still needs a reconciliation/closure process for those amounts.
- The initial measurement contexts listed in the schema cover the pilot's needs; any new context will receive its own typed FK and batch-matching constraints rather than reuse a polymorphic reference.
- Allocation review is initially modeled at complete-set level. If participants require independent beneficiary-row approvals or disputes, the review model must be adjusted before that workflow is built.
- Initial transactions use one selected currency per trade/sale; payment and event currencies match it. There is no KES default and no conversion in the proposed MVP. A future conversion requires an explicitly recorded original and converted amount, both currency codes, exchange rate, timestamp and evidence/source.
- The currency reference/precision policy and PostgreSQL implementation of deferred constraints/triggers remain to be proven before migrations are approved; no application or database behavior is implemented in Phase 0.
- The proposed per-aggregate streams cover the transaction domains participants need to inspect; stream boundaries remain subject to pilot review.
- An independent party is willing and able to hold signed checkpoints if tamper evidence beyond the database operator is required.
- A user can be provisioned to a real person/party without collecting unnecessary identity data.
- QR labels can be attached/scanned reliably under site conditions and do not create a safety or privacy issue.
- The proposed event model can represent local variations using optional fields/configuration without turning unverified practices into mandatory rules.
- Pooled residue and recovered gold can be traced to contributing batches even where exact per-source recovered quantities cannot be calculated.
- A permissioned network has benefit only if multiple independent organizations participate in governance and validation.
